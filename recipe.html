JOLLOF RICE RECIPE 1

3 cups of rice, serves 3 to 4 person, 2 tomata paste

Recipe Information

    Ingredients

    1. 1kg Chicken/beef 2. 1000ml of Ground Fresh tomatoes. 3. 50ml Ground Fresh peppers 4. 1 Teaspoon Curry powder 5. 1 teaspoon Thyme 6. 1teaspoon Nutmeg powder 7. 2 seasoning cubes 8. 4 cups of rice

Instructions

  1. step 1: Blend the Base>
  2. step 2: Fry the Tomato Paste + Base>
  3. step 3: Season It Up
  4. step 4: Add the Rice + Steam
  5. Step 5: Fluff + Serve

TIPS

Dont skip rinsing the rice—it prevents sogginess Adjust heat based on your spice tolerance KEEP WATER JUST ALITTLE ABOVE THE RICE
